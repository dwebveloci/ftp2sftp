---
name: compact-handoff
description: Produces a compact continuation summary before context compaction or a new session.
---

Produce a concise engineering handoff containing:

- Goal.
- Current behavior.
- Confirmed findings.
- Decisions made.
- Files changed.
- Commands and test results.
- Remaining work.
- Risks.
- Exact next action.

Exclude tutorials, repeated prompts and discarded speculation.
Keep it under 800 words.
